# Troop Training Requirement

A Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord mod (1.4.8, War Sails compatible). **Work in progress - not released yet.**

You can only upgrade a troop into a unit whose combat skills someone in the party can teach. For each combat skill
(One Handed, Two Handed, Polearm, Bow, Crossbow, Throwing, Riding, Athletics), the upgraded troop may not exceed the
best value among you and the companions travelling with you. Want elite archers? Learn the bow, or bring someone who
has. Recruiting already-trained troops is unaffected.

Nothing is saved into your campaign: the mod can be removed at any time.

## Requirements

- [Harmony](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2006)
- [Mod Configuration Menu](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/612) - optional, for the settings
