# Troop Training Requirement

A Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord mod (1.4.8, War Sails compatible). **Work in progress - not released yet.**

You can only upgrade a troop into a unit whose combat skills someone in the party can teach. For each combat skill
(One Handed, Two Handed, Polearm, Bow, Crossbow, Throwing, Riding, Athletics), the upgraded troop may not exceed the
best value among you and the companions travelling with you. Want elite archers? Learn the bow, or bring someone who
has. Recruiting already-trained troops is unaffected.

On the party screen a blocked upgrade button is disabled (orange frame) and its tooltip says why, e.g. "Requires
Bow 120 - your best is 64 (Cadan)". In the encyclopedia, a unit's troop tree marks in orange the units your party
can't upgrade into yet, with the same reasons in their tooltip.

Nothing is saved into your campaign: the mod can be removed at any time.

## Requirements

- [Harmony](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2006)
- [Mod Configuration Menu](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/612) - optional, for the settings

## Compatibility

Compatible with [Troop Equipment Upgrade](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3674824323), and
works very nicely with it (and Culture Equipment Seeder): gear has to be bought and the skill has to be taught, so
better troops are earned - a slower, more rewarding progression.

The training check always comes first: an upgrade your party can't train is refused before Troop Equipment Upgrade
reserves or buys any gear, so you never pay for an upgrade that didn't happen. Load order doesn't matter.
